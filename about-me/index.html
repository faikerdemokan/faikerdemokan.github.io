About Me, I'm a Brand Designer...

I'm a Brand Designer and Creative Director with almost 30 years of experience at the intersection of design, brand strategy, and digital growth.

For me, design isn't just about looking good — it's about solving business problems. After 30 years at the craft table, I've learned this: A true professional doesn't just come up with the idea; they execute it flawlessly. With pixel-perfect, press-ready, and code-ready craftsmanship built to last.

My career started in 1997 at Sabah Newspaper in page design, color separation, and ad design — a foundation that gave me discipline, typography, and a deep respect for the grid. That print-rooted discipline took me to leading creative agencies, where I led creative teams, and to serving as Art Director for major national industrial brands, where I oversaw everything from corporate identity and packaging to 360° campaigns across TV, radio, and digital.

From 2010 to 2017, I worked as an external Creative Director and Brand Consultant for a diverse portfolio of national and international corporate clients. During this period, I partnered with companies across industrial, retail, legal, and service sectors — leading full-scale rebranding operations, building brand identities from the ground up, architecting e-commerce ecosystems and SEO infrastructures, and directing launch films and integrated campaign assets.

Since 2020, I've been running my own creative studio, Creamac — operating as Founder, Creative Director & Digital Brand Strategist, providing end-to-end consultancy for corporate brands.

What I do today is fully integrated. I own the entire pipeline from concept to final delivery — crafting the creative concept, obsessing over the details in design execution, and supervising final production. My skill set covers the fundamentals — brand identity, editorial design, book and catalog design, packaging — and extends to modern essentials — video editing and post-production, motion design, photography, UI design, and AI-powered production.

Then I connect that creative output to performance — integrating Google Ads, Social Media Strategy, Analytics, SEO, Email and Display Marketing to make sure brands don't just get seen, they scale.
