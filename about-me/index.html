So, about me? I'm a Brand Architect.

I’m a Creative Director and Brand Designer with nearly three decades of experience at the intersection of design, strategy, and digital growth.

For me, great branding isn’t about noise or surface-level aesthetics — it’s about clarity, longevity, and solving real business problems. After nearly 30 years at the craft table, I’ve learned one fundamental truth: A true professional doesn’t just generate ideas; they execute them flawlessly. With pixel-perfect, press-ready, and code-ready craftsmanship built to last.

The Foundation & Evolution
My journey began in 1997 at Sabah Newspaper, mastering page layout, color separation, and print production. That early exposure to tight deadlines and technical limits instilled in me an unshakeable design discipline, a mastery of typography, and a deep respect for the grid.

Over the years, that print-rooted foundation evolved into leading creative teams as Art Director for major industrial brands and creative agencies. I’ve overseen 360° campaigns spanning TV, radio, digital, packaging, and corporate identities.

Between 2010 and 2017, I served as an external Creative Director and Brand Consultant for a diverse portfolio of national and international corporate clients across industrial, retail, legal, and service sectors — directing full-scale rebrands, building e-commerce ecosystems, architecting SEO infrastructures, and producing launch films.

Since 2020, I have been leading my own creative studio, Creamac, as Founder, Creative Director & Digital Brand Strategist — providing end-to-end brand consultancy for corporate clients.

Full-Pipeline Craftsmanship
What I deliver today is fully integrated. I own the entire pipeline from concept to final delivery:
